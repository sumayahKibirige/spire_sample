A Message from the Headteacher

For nearly two decades, Spire High School has pursued a singular purpose: to cultivate young men and women of uncommon intellectual courage and moral clarity. We are convinced that excellence, properly understood, is never merely academic.

Whether in the classroom, on the sports field, or in the arts, our students discover that the standards they set for themselves here become the foundations of everything they achieve beyond these walls. We are delighted you are considering joining us.

Mr. David Ochieng Headteacher, M.Ed. in Educational Leadership
